# Content outline: community aquatics club

This outline preserves the reference site's broad information architecture while using original, replaceable placeholder guidance. Do not reuse reference-site copy or branding.

## Home

1. **Identity header** — Use a new organization name, a short statement of community role, and optionally an authorized logo.
2. **Priority links** — Offer clear paths to membership, facilities, programs, club information, and current notices.
3. **Urgent update** — Add a concise current announcement with an effective date and a route to details.
4. **Facility status and enrollment** — State current opening status, enrollment availability, and per-location contact/action details.
5. **Club news and impact** — Publish short, dated updates about milestones, facility improvements, community projects, and partners.
6. **Benefits teaser** — Introduce activity benefits with original supporting text and a link to a fuller article.
7. **Continuation and support** — Link to the full activity catalog and show authorized partner acknowledgments with alt text.

Suggested neutral placeholder: “Find a program, check a facility, or see what's happening at your local aquatic club.”

## Services index

1. **Activity catalog** — Group beginner/family lessons, fitness classes, rehabilitation, personal coaching, events, and competitive programs as relevant.
2. **Service details links** — Each category should lead to an accessible page with audience, schedule, location, and enrollment information.
3. **Return navigation** — Provide a labeled link back to the home page.

## Informational poster/article

1. **Topic introduction** — State the topic in live, accessible text.
2. **Core information** — Provide brief evidence-based explanation and practical next steps.
3. **Optional visual** — Use an original or licensed image only when useful; never make essential information available only inside an image.
4. **Return navigation** — Link back to the relevant activities or home page.

## Club update

1. **Dated update heading** — Identify the milestone or seasonal update and its date.
2. **Update summary** — Explain what changed and why it matters to members.
3. **Optional media and partner credit** — Add approved imagery and accurate attribution when applicable.
4. **Return navigation** — Provide a labeled route back to club information.

## Sustainability or facility project

1. **Project summary** — Explain the facility improvement in plain language.
2. **Impact** — Describe the community or environmental relevance without unsupported claims.
3. **Partner attribution** — Identify funders or collaborators only with verified names and permission.
4. **Return navigation** — Link back to club information or facilities.

## Operational notice

1. **Notice heading and date** — Clearly identify the service change and when it applies.
2. **Affected locations/services** — List exactly what is open, closed, or changed.
3. **Visitor next step** — Provide an accessible contact or status link.
4. **Return navigation** — Provide a labeled back link.

## Location status and enrollment offer

1. **Campaign summary** — Use newly written offer terms, valid dates, eligibility, and conditions.
2. **Location status** — State availability for each facility as text.
3. **Enrollment action** — Use a direct signup or contact call to action.
4. **Return navigation** — Link to the home page.

Avoid hardcoding time-sensitive terms or dates into static poster graphics; keep them as editable text and include expiry information.

# Content outline: OSNO Site reference template

This outline abstracts the reference site's structure into an original template for a service business. Replace every example with new, verified content for the implementing organization.

## Home

1. **Navigation** — Use a compact brand mark, links to the key sections, an optional language switcher, a utility/login route when applicable, and one primary contact action.
2. **Hero promise** — State what is delivered, who it helps, and what the customer controls afterward. Pair a high-contrast action with a lower-commitment exploration action.
3. **Proof example** — Show one representative outcome in desktop and mobile frames. Add a short project label and a permitted external case-study link.
4. **Process** — Explain three or four stages from discovery through delivery and ongoing ownership. Keep each stage to one clear action and outcome.
5. **Capability grid** — Group the service's practical benefits into cards. Mix simple icons with original diagrams or interface illustrations; avoid copying source screenshots.
6. **Management or product showcase** — If the service includes a tool or dashboard, show a simplified original mockup beside the resulting customer experience.
7. **Conversion band** — Reinforce the next step with a short invitation, expectation-setting copy, and one strong action.
8. **Footer** — Provide contact details, secondary navigation, language or legal links, ownership information, and any approved partner badge.

Suggested neutral placeholder: “A clear, useful service for teams that want a better presence without adding unnecessary complexity.”

## Contact

1. **Introduction** — Tell visitors what happens after they submit the form and provide a direct contact method when appropriate.
2. **Enquiry form** — Ask only for the details needed to respond: name, email, organization or business, message, and explicit consent.
3. **Submission feedback** — Expose loading, success, and error states through text and an `aria-live` status region.
4. **Return route** — Provide a labeled link back to the home page.
5. **Shared footer** — Keep the same contact and navigation context as the landing page.

## Content and implementation guardrails

- Use original copy; do not reuse the reference site's slogans, feature wording, or case-study text.
- Use an original hero image, licensed media, or a CSS/illustration treatment rather than the source's photographic assets.
- Keep claims measurable and organization-specific. Avoid promising search ranking, speed, translation quality, or ownership terms without evidence.
- Make all time-sensitive pricing, availability, and service terms editable text rather than poster graphics.
- If multilingual support is included, provide equivalent content for each locale and identify the current language accessibly.

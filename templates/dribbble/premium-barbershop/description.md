This is a **premium barbershop website** with a strong masculine, vintage-modern aesthetic. It appears designed for a barbershop called **“METT”** and focuses on showcasing the shop’s services, experience, barbers, and customer results.

### Overall visual style
- **Color palette:** dark navy/black, warm beige/cream, and gold/brown accents.
- **Typography:** bold, rounded/display fonts for headings combined with clean sans-serif text.
- **Photography:** large, atmospheric photos of barbers cutting hair and customers getting styled.
- **Style:** premium, traditional barbershop mixed with contemporary branding.
- **Layout:** long single-page landing page with alternating dark and light sections.
- **CTA:** frequent **“Book an Appointment”** buttons encourage visitors to schedule a service.

### Main sections

1. **Hero**
   - Large full-width barbershop photograph.
   - Logo at the top-left.
   - Navigation such as Home, About, Services, etc.
   - Main headline: **“Make your own Style”**
   - Supporting copy and a prominent booking CTA.

2. **About / Experience**
   - Highlights **“15 years of Experience.”**
   - Explains the barbershop's history and philosophy.
   - Includes a circular barber/customer photograph.
   - Shows business hours and another appointment CTA.

3. **Services**
   - Section titled **“What we provide to our customer.”**
   - Service cards with icons and prices:
     - Beard Trimming
     - Clean Shave
     - Hair's Cut
     - Face Wash
   - Each card uses a minimalist cream/gold design.

4. **Hair & Beard section**
   - Large barber/customer image.
   - Messaging around taking care of customers' hair and beard.
   - Bullet points emphasizing professional service and quality.

5. **Customer satisfaction**
   - Dark section emphasizing the shop's commitment to satisfied customers.
   - Includes another large photograph and location/business information.

6. **Team / Barbers**
   - **“Meet the artists”** section.
   - Displays individual barber cards with photographs, names, and roles such as Senior Barber / Junior Barber.
   - Social icons are included on the cards.

7. **Newsletter**
   - Gold/brown promotional banner.
   - **“Get 10% Discount”** incentive for subscribing.
   - Email input and subscription button.

8. **Testimonials**
   - **“Our lovely clients”**
   - Large testimonial card with customer feedback.
   - Carousel/navigation controls suggest multiple testimonials.

9. **Tips & Tricks**
   - Three article/content cards.
   - Uses barbering imagery and short article titles.
   - Likely intended to support SEO and demonstrate expertise.

10. **Footer**
   - Dark footer with columns for:
     - Links
     - Business Hours
     - Company
     - Contact information
   - Social/contact information and copyright.

### Brand positioning

The website is clearly trying to position the barbershop as **more than a basic haircut business**. The messaging emphasizes:

> **Experience + craftsmanship + premium service + personal style**

The combination of **professional photography, gold accents, testimonials, team profiles, pricing, and prominent appointment CTAs** gives it the feel of a higher-end local service business.

For a barbershop, this is a **strong structure to use as a reference for your own client websites**, especially because the page follows a logical conversion funnel:

**Visual impression → credibility → services/prices → expertise → team → social proof → content → booking.**
